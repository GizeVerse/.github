# GizeVerse

**Small digital characters that live alongside you, built in the open.**

GizeVerse is a home for playful companions: characters with their own behaviour that share your screen without getting in your way. Each project is free and open source, honest about what it can do, and careful with your privacy.

## Projects

| Project | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [**GizeCat**](https://github.com/GizeVerse/GizeCat) | One animated, persistent cat that lives on your desktop: it wanders, naps, climbs windows, hunts your cursor, and can be petted, picked up and thrown, all without blocking your clicks. | Windows 10/11; web host; macOS and Linux foundations ([platform matrix](https://github.com/GizeVerse/GizeCat/blob/main/docs/gizeverse/capability-matrix.md)) |

## How we build

- **Honest about platforms.** A platform is listed as supported only after the app has actually run there. Everything else is labelled as implemented-but-unverified, simulated or blocked, and each project's capability matrix says which.
- **Private by default.** No telemetry, no keyboard hooks. Any screen or AI features are off until you opt in.
- **Original art.** The characters are drawn in code; no third-party assets.

## Contributing

Ideas, bug reports, platform reports (especially from macOS and Linux desktops we cannot test ourselves) and pull requests are welcome. Start with a project's `CONTRIBUTING.md` and its issue templates.
